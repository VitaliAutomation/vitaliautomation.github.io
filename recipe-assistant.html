Recipe Assistant

Find, import, organise, save and scale recipes without the unnecessary complexity of traditional recipe-management apps.

A recipe-first experience

Recipe Assistant is designed around one simple goal: make working with recipes easy. Find recipes online, import them from different sources, review and edit the result, save your favourites and automatically scale ingredient quantities when you need a different portion size.

Features

Smart Recipe Search

Search for useful recipes while filtering out irrelevant pages and poor-quality results.

Website Import

Import recipes from supported websites and turn them into clean, organised ingredients and cooking steps.

Photo & Image Import

Turn recipe photos, screenshots and other images into editable recipes.

AI Assistance

Intelligent assistance helps find, interpret and organise recipe information.

Recipe Scaling

Change the recipe quantity and automatically recalculate ingredient amounts.

Friendly Recipe Library

Save, search, edit and organise recipes in a clean interface designed for everyday use.
