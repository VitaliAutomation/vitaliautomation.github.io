Recipe Assistant Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Privacy summary

Recipe Assistant is designed to be privacy-friendly and local-first. Vitali Automation does not require a Recipe Assistant account, does not sell personal information, does not display advertising, and does not use users' recipes for advertising purposes.

1. About this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Recipe Assistant, developed and published by Vitali Automation, accesses, uses, stores and shares information when you use the application.

Developer: Vitali Automation
Contact: vitaliautomation@gmail.com

2. User Accounts

Recipe Assistant does not currently require users to create a Recipe Assistant account or provide a name, email address, password or other profile information in order to use the app.

3. Recipes and App Data

Recipes created, imported, edited and saved in Recipe Assistant are primarily stored locally on the user's device.

This may include:

  • recipe names and descriptions;
  • ingredients and quantities;
  • cooking instructions;
  • serving or scaling information;
  • recipe categories and other recipe settings;
  • selected recipe images;
  • app preferences and Free/Pro feature-use information.

Vitali Automation does not operate a cloud recipe-storage service for the current version of Recipe Assistant and does not automatically upload a user's personal recipe library to Vitali Automation servers.

4. Camera, Photos and Images

Recipe Assistant may allow users to take a photo or select an existing image for the purpose of creating or importing a recipe.

The app accesses only the photo or image selected or captured by the user for this feature. Recipe Assistant does not use camera or photo access for advertising, profiling or background monitoring.

Image and text recognition used by the current app is designed to be processed locally on the device. Vitali Automation does not operate a server that receives or stores users' recipe photographs or screenshots.

5. AI Assistance

Recipe Assistant includes intelligent features that help users search for, interpret, organise and import recipe information.

The app's internal recipe-processing and assistance features do not require users to submit personal information to Vitali Automation. Personal recipe libraries are not used by Vitali Automation to train advertising profiles or sold to third parties.

6. Online Recipe Search and Website Import

Some Recipe Assistant features require internet access. When the user chooses to search for recipes online or import a recipe from a website, the app makes network requests to search providers, websites or other online sources needed to perform that request.

Information transmitted as part of these requests may include:

  • the recipe search terms entered by the user;
  • the web address (URL) selected for recipe import;
  • standard internet connection information required to communicate with websites, such as the user's IP address and browser/app request information.

These third-party websites and search services may process network information according to their own privacy policies and terms. Vitali Automation does not control the privacy practices of external websites.

7. Google Play and Lifetime Pro Purchases

Recipe Assistant may offer a one-time Lifetime Pro purchase through Google Play.

Payments are processed by Google Play. Vitali Automation does not receive or store the user's full payment-card or banking details.

Recipe Assistant may receive purchase-status and entitlement information from Google Play that is necessary to confirm whether Lifetime Pro has been purchased and to restore Pro access when appropriate.

Google's handling of payment and account information is governed by Google's own privacy policy and Google Play terms.

8. Advertising

Recipe Assistant does not contain third-party advertising and does not use advertising SDKs to track users for targeted advertising.

9. Analytics and Tracking

The current version of Recipe Assistant does not intentionally collect personal usage analytics for Vitali Automation and does not use user activity to build advertising profiles.

Google Play and the Android operating system may provide standard installation, performance, purchase or diagnostic information as part of their normal platform services. Such information is handled according to Google's applicable policies.

10. Permissions

Recipe Assistant requests only device access that is required for features chosen by the user. Depending on the Android version and features used, this may include:

  • Internet access, for online recipe search, website importing and Google Play services;
  • Camera access, when the user chooses to photograph a recipe;
  • Access to a user-selected photo or image, when importing a recipe from an existing image.

Camera and image access is used only when the user chooses the corresponding feature.

11. Data Sharing

Vitali Automation does not sell users' personal information.

Information may leave the device only where necessary to provide a feature requested by the user, such as communicating with an external recipe website, search service or Google Play.

Recipe Assistant does not automatically publish users' personal recipes or make their recipe libraries available to other Recipe Assistant users.

12. Data Security

Vitali Automation designs Recipe Assistant to minimise unnecessary collection and transmission of user information. Where the app communicates with supported internet services, standard secure network connections are used where available.

Users should also protect their Android device with appropriate device security, as locally stored recipes and app information are protected by the security of the user's device and operating system.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored recipes and associated app data remain on the user's device until they are deleted by the user, removed through an app feature, cleared through Android app-storage settings, or removed when the app is uninstalled, subject to Android backup or restore behaviour.

Because Recipe Assistant currently does not require a Vitali Automation user account or maintain a developer-operated cloud recipe library, there is normally no separate Recipe Assistant online account for the user to request deletion of.

Information associated with Google Play purchases is retained and handled by Google according to Google's own legal, accounting and platform requirements.

14. Children's Privacy

Recipe Assistant is a general-purpose recipe application and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Vitali Automation does not knowingly use Recipe Assistant to collect personal information from children for advertising or profiling.

15. External Websites

Recipe Assistant may display links to, search results from, or recipe content originating from third-party websites. Those websites operate independently from Vitali Automation and may have their own privacy policies, cookies and data-processing practices.

Users should review the privacy information provided by any third-party website they choose to visit.

16. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Recipe Assistant gains new features, changes the way information is processed, or when legal or Google Play requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
