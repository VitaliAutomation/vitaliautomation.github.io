Recipe Assistant Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Privacy summary

Recipe Assistant is designed to be privacy-friendly and local-first. It does not require a Recipe Assistant account, does not contain advertising, does not use advertising trackers, and does not operate a Vitali Automation cloud service for users' personal recipe libraries.

Recipes and selected recipe photos are normally stored on the device. Online search and website-import features necessarily communicate with third-party websites and search providers. Photo text recognition uses Google ML Kit on-device: the photo and recognised text are not sent to Google for recognition, although ML Kit sends limited diagnostic and usage metrics to Google as described below.

1. Who we are

Recipe Assistant is developed and published by Vitali Automation. This Privacy Policy explains how the app accesses, uses, stores and transmits information.

Privacy and support contact: vitaliautomation@gmail.com

2. Accounts and information we do not require

Recipe Assistant does not currently require a Vitali Automation account. Users do not need to provide Vitali Automation with a name, email address, password, telephone number or profile in order to use the app.

The app does not intentionally collect precise location, contacts, calendar data, SMS messages, microphone/audio recordings, health data or advertising IDs.

3. Recipes and app data stored on the device

The app stores recipe and configuration data locally in app-private storage. This can include:

  • recipe names, descriptions, ingredients, quantities and cooking steps;
  • serving, scaling, category and favourite information;
  • recipe source URLs and remote image URLs where applicable;
  • locally saved recipe photos;
  • app language, settings and search-provider configuration;
  • Free-plan usage counters; and
  • Google Play Lifetime Pro entitlement/verification information needed by the app.

Vitali Automation does not currently operate a server that automatically receives or stores users' personal recipe libraries.

4. Camera, gallery and recipe images

Recipe Assistant lets a user choose an existing image or take a photo for recipe creation and photo-to-recipe import. The current Android app does not request broad photo-library or storage access in its own manifest. It uses Android/system selection or camera flows and receives the image that the user selects or captures.

Selected images may be held temporarily while the user reviews or crops them. If the user saves an image as a recipe photo, Recipe Assistant copies it into the app's private storage. Deleting the corresponding local recipe photo from the app removes the app's stored copy where that deletion is supported.

5. Photo text recognition (OCR) and Google ML Kit

Recipe Assistant uses Google ML Kit Text Recognition to recognise text in recipe photos and screenshots. The image content and the text produced by ML Kit are processed on the device for text recognition and are not sent to Google servers for recognition.

Google states that ML Kit may contact Google services for items such as updates, bug fixes and hardware-accelerator compatibility, and that ML Kit automatically sends limited diagnostics and usage metrics. Depending on the ML Kit feature and platform, those metrics can include device and app information, a per-installation identifier, performance/latency information, API configuration such as image format or resolution, input/output size, feature version, feature events and error codes. Google states that this telemetry is encrypted in transit and is not transferred by ML Kit to other third parties.

Vitali Automation does not receive the recipe image, recognised recipe text, or ML Kit diagnostic telemetry on a Vitali Automation server.

More information: Google ML Kit Terms & Privacy and ML Kit data disclosure guidance.

6. AI Assistant

The current Recipe Assistant AI Assistant does not send a user's personal recipe library or recipe photographs to an external generative-AI/LLM service. Recipe interpretation, ranking and cleanup logic is performed by the app.

When the user chooses an online search function, however, the search text is transmitted to the enabled web search providers so that internet search results can be returned. This network search is described in the next section.

7. Online recipe search

Online recipe search is optional and only runs when the user chooses to use that feature. Recipe Assistant may send the user's search text to more than one enabled search provider. The built-in provider list currently includes services such as DuckDuckGo, Bing, Yahoo and Mojeek for automatic searching, with additional providers available for manual/browser use. Users can change enabled providers in the app's Search Provider settings.

A search provider can receive the search query and ordinary network-request information such as the sender's IP address, app/browser request headers and language preference. Search providers process this information under their own privacy policies and retention practices.

Users should avoid putting unnecessary personal or sensitive information into an online recipe-search query because the query is sent to the enabled third-party search provider(s).

8. Automatic candidate checking and website recipe import

To find useful recipe results and reject search pages, articles or other unsuitable results, Recipe Assistant may automatically request candidate recipe web pages after an online search. When the user imports a recipe by URL, the app also requests that URL and analyses the returned page.

Those websites can receive normal connection information such as the user's IP address, request headers and language preference. The app may store the recipe source URL locally with the saved recipe.

Built-in search providers use HTTPS, and the current Android release target does not opt in to cleartext HTTP traffic. Users who add custom search-provider templates should use HTTPS.

9. In-app browser, external websites and cookies

Recipe Assistant includes an in-app web browser for navigating recipe and search websites. Pages in this browser are third-party web content. Those websites may use JavaScript, cookies, local storage and similar web technologies and may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

When the user chooses Import current page, Recipe Assistant reads the currently displayed page content so it can extract recipe information. Recipe Assistant does not provide third-party websites with access to the user's local Recipe Assistant database.

10. Remote recipe images

Recipes imported from websites may include an image URL from the source site. Recipe Assistant normally attempts to save an imported recipe image locally. If a remote image remains linked instead, displaying that image can make a request to the third-party image host, which can receive ordinary connection information such as the user's IP address and request headers.

11. Clipboard, backup and restore

Recipe Assistant can read text from the system clipboard when the user explicitly chooses a paste action.

Normal backups are now created as portable .rabackup files. When the user chooses Create backup file, Android's system file picker lets the user choose where the file is saved. The backup can contain the user's recipes, ingredients, cooking steps, favourites, source links and locally stored recipe photos. Built-in sample recipes are not included because the app recreates them.

When the user chooses Restore backup file, Android's system file picker lets the user select the backup. Recipe Assistant validates the file before restoring it and copies included recipe photos back into the app's private storage. Existing recipes are kept and exact duplicates are skipped.

Vitali Automation does not automatically receive or upload backup files. If the user chooses to save a backup to a third-party or cloud storage location such as Google Drive, that storage provider processes the selected file according to its own terms and privacy policy.

12. Android backup and device transfer

Recipe Assistant does not run a Vitali Automation cloud-sync service. Android and device manufacturers may provide operating-system backup, restore or device-to-device migration features for app data depending on the user's Android version, device, account and backup settings. Those platform services are controlled by the operating system/provider rather than by Vitali Automation.

Users who do not want platform backup or device transfer should review their Android/device backup settings. Recipe Assistant's user-created .rabackup files are separate from Android's operating-system backup and are created only when the user explicitly chooses Create backup file.

13. Google Play Billing and Lifetime Pro

Recipe Assistant may offer a one-time Lifetime Pro purchase through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment transaction. Recipe Assistant does not receive or store the user's full payment-card or bank-account details.

The app receives purchase status and verification/entitlement information required to determine whether Lifetime Pro has been purchased or restored. In the current app architecture this entitlement information is stored locally on the device. Google processes Google Play account, transaction and payment information under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

14. Advertising, developer analytics and tracking

Recipe Assistant does not contain third-party advertising, does not use an advertising SDK and does not use advertising IDs for targeted advertising.

The current app does not include Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Sentry, Mixpanel, Amplitude or a similar developer-added analytics/crash-reporting service. This does not mean that no technical data ever leaves the device: Google ML Kit sends the limited diagnostic and usage metrics described in section 5, and online features communicate with the third-party services selected or required for those features.

15. Permissions and device access

The current Android app declares Internet access and Google Play Billing access. It does not declare permissions for precise/coarse location, contacts, microphone/audio recording, SMS, phone state, advertising ID, or broad photo/storage-library access.

Camera and photo selection are initiated only when the user chooses the corresponding feature, using Android/system selection or camera mechanisms.

16. Security

Recipe Assistant is designed to minimise unnecessary collection and transmission. Local recipes are stored in the app's private Android storage. Built-in online providers use HTTPS and the Android build does not opt in to cleartext network traffic. Data sent by Google ML Kit is encrypted in transit according to Google's ML Kit disclosure documentation.

No internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should keep Android and their device security up to date.

17. Retention and deletion

Local recipe and app data remains on the device until it is deleted through app functions, Android app-storage controls, device migration/backup handling, or app removal, subject to the behaviour of Android backup and restore services.

Because Recipe Assistant currently has no Vitali Automation user account and no Vitali Automation cloud recipe database, Vitali Automation normally has no server-side personal recipe library to delete.

Data processed by Google Play, Google ML Kit, search providers, websites or other third-party services is subject to those providers' retention and deletion policies.

Privacy or deletion questions can be sent to vitaliautomation@gmail.com.

18. Support emails

If a user voluntarily emails Vitali Automation for support or feedback, Vitali Automation receives the sender's email address and the information included in that message. It is used to respond to the enquiry, diagnose the reported issue and maintain necessary support records. Users should not include unnecessary sensitive information in support messages.

19. Legal bases where UK data-protection law applies

Where Vitali Automation acts as a controller of personal data under UK data-protection law, it processes only what is necessary for the relevant purpose. Support emails and similar voluntary enquiries are processed on the basis of Vitali Automation's legitimate interests in responding to users, supporting the app and maintaining appropriate records. Information needed to provide or restore a paid Lifetime Pro entitlement is processed where necessary to provide the purchased service, although the payment itself is handled by Google Play.

Optional online search, website import, camera/photo selection and clipboard features are initiated by the user. Where consent or device permission is required by applicable law or Android, the relevant choice or permission is requested at the point of use. Third-party search providers, websites, Google, GitHub and other independent services determine their own legal bases for the information they process under their own privacy notices.

20. International processing

Third-party services used or contacted by optional online features may process connection information, search queries or service telemetry in countries outside the United Kingdom. Those providers are responsible for describing their international-processing arrangements and safeguards in their own privacy notices. Vitali Automation does not operate an overseas cloud server containing users' personal Recipe Assistant libraries in the current app.

21. Data protection rights

Where UK or other applicable data-protection law gives users rights over personal data processed by Vitali Automation, those rights may include access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability, depending on the circumstances and legal basis for the processing.

Most Recipe Assistant content is held only on the user's device and can be edited or deleted directly by the user. For information actually held by Vitali Automation, such as a support email, a privacy request can be sent to vitaliautomation@gmail.com.

UK users also have the right to raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) .

22. Children's privacy

Recipe Assistant is a general-purpose recipe application and is not designed to collect personal information from children for advertising, profiling or social-networking purposes. Vitali Automation does not knowingly sell or use children's personal data for advertising.

23. Vitali Automation website

Vitali Automation's website and this Privacy Policy are hosted using GitHub Pages. Vitali Automation does not currently place its own advertising or analytics scripts on these static pages. GitHub may process normal website connection and service information under GitHub's own privacy terms.

See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

24. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if Recipe Assistant's data practices materially change, for example if a future release introduces a developer-operated cloud service, external AI service, advertising, additional analytics or other processing not described here. The revision date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy changes.
